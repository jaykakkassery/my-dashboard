HotelBeds/BedsOnline is collapsing, and it's the real story here

Combined HB_APITUDE + BEDS_ONLINE consumer bookings fell 56% to 76% YoY every single month, and the decline is getting worse, not better — from -56% in April to -76% in August. That is a far bigger swing than anything else on this page, Sabre's growth included. ATI moved in a noisy but roughly flat band (-24% to +24%, no clear direction).

Sabre is still the one provider with consumer bookings clearly ahead of last year (peaking +51% in August), and Rapid and Priceline were flat-to-declining as noted above. But next to HotelBeds/BedsOnline's collapse, Sabre's gain looks smaller in context: the combined total was down YoY from April through July and only turned positive in August, and HotelBeds/BedsOnline's ongoing decline is the biggest single drag on that total — worth investigating on its own before reading too much into the provider-replacement story.
